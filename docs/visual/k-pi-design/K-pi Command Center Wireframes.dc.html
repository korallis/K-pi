4All eight changes — one vocabulary, one key map, a labelled input line, the stuck and ship screens, autopilot, first run, revisions, 80 columns

The plain words are now the only words; technical names appear dimmed, underneath, in details. Every key means one thing everywhere. The input line says what typing does. 1b and 1d are retired as home candidates; 4b is home.

Vocabulary. understand · plan · ◆ you · build · test · review · ◆ you · ship. Autopilot drops the two ◆ stops.
Keys. ↵ deeper · esc back · tab next for you · s stop · ? help, everywhere. a r v e when it asks. g c b u when it's stuck. No letter means two things.
Input line. Always prefixed with its mode: new job › · steer build › · reply to plan › · guide build ›.
Width. Designed at 108 columns. Under 100 the rail collapses to glyphs and keys to letters. 80 is the floor (4k).
